About us

A bistro built for the way Kampala actually eats.

We're not a chain and we're not trying to be. Donel's is a single kitchen on Ntinda–Kisaasi Road, run by people who live and work in this city.

How we got here

Donel's started with a simple observation: in Kampala you can find good food, or you can find quick food, or you can find a decent place to sit — but rarely all three in the same building.

So we built one. A kitchen that cooks to order instead of pre-plating. A bar that's properly stocked and open late. A room that's bright enough to work in during the day and relaxed enough to stay in at night.

We're on Ntinda–Kisaasi Road, directly opposite Capital Shoppers — easy to find whether you're coming from Ntinda, Kisaasi, Bukoto or Naguru, and easy to park once you get here.

How we operate

Three rules, and we don't bend them.

Cook it when it's ordered

Nothing is pre-plated and left waiting. Your order hits the kitchen when you place it, and it comes out when it's ready.

Price it honestly

Our food and drinks menus are published online. You see the price before you order — no surprises at the end of the meal.

Respect people's time

Delivery riders leave when the food is packed. Takeaway is bagged before you arrive. Tables are ready when we say they are.

Keep the room usable

Free WiFi, backup power, comfortable seating and enough space between tables that a meeting doesn't become a shouting match.
